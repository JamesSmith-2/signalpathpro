What to expect

From first conversation to handoff

  1. Define the job

    Start with the date or deadline, the room, your team, and what needs to work.

  2. Agree on scope

    Identify what can stay, what needs testing or changes, and who owns each part of the job.

  3. Test and hand off

    Check the signal path and controls. For recurring setups, leave practical notes your team can use.

Good to know

Common questions

Can you work with our existing equipment?

Yes. Start with what you have. If an upgrade makes sense, James can help separate the necessary changes from the nice-to-haves.

Can we bring you in for one event or one problem?

Yes. Tell James if you need a one-off show, a check of a specific system, or support for a recurring program so you can agree on the right scope.

Do you work with volunteer teams?

Yes. Training and clear operator documentation can be part of the project, especially when several people share the booth.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the scope, time, and equipment involved. Share a budget range if you have one, so the first conversation can focus on what’s realistic.
