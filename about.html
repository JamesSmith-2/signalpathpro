My approach

Make the system easier to run

My background is in sound design and media production. My hands-on work includes live audio, multi-camera livestreams, and supporting church tech teams that need the booth to work week after week.

I start by asking what’s already in the room and who has to operate it. Sometimes the answer is better routing, a reliable way to check the stream, or written instructions. If new equipment is the right answer, I want the team to know what problem it solves.

What I work on

  • Live sound, patching, and show operation
  • Livestream signal flow and troubleshooting
  • AV planning, checklists, and training
